Mastering Paraphrasing

Step-by-Step Guided Learning for English Majors

🧠 Part 1: Global Understanding

Q1. Read the sentence and choose the best answer: What is the main idea of this sentence?

"All are important and must be reasonably satisfied if we are to fulfill our biological destiny."
  • A. Humans should satisfy all their desires as much as possible.
  • B. All basic needs are important and should be properly met.
  • C. Only biological needs are important for humans.
  • D. People should focus only on survival and reproduction.
🧑‍🏫 Detailed Explanation:
A ❌：误把 reasonably 理解为“越多越好”
B ✅：正确表达“重要 + 合理满足”
C ❌：忽略其他四种需求
D ❌：只强调第一种需求

Q2.1 Decide whether the statement is TRUE or FALSE: "All" refers to all human activities.

"All are important and must be reasonably satisfied if we are to fulfill our biological destiny."
  • TRUE
  • FALSE
🧑‍🏫 Explanation: ❌：“All”指五种需求，不是所有活动。

Q2.2 Decide whether the statement is TRUE or FALSE: The sentence suggests that too much satisfaction is acceptable.

"All are important and must be reasonably satisfied if we are to fulfill our biological destiny."
  • TRUE
  • FALSE
🧑‍🏫 Explanation: ❌：“reasonably”直接排除了“过度（overindulgence）”。

Q2.3 Decide whether the statement is TRUE or FALSE: The sentence emphasizes balance in meeting needs.

"All are important and must be reasonably satisfied if we are to fulfill our biological destiny."
  • TRUE
  • FALSE
🧑‍🏫 Explanation: ✅：核心句意确实是强调合理平衡地满足需求。

🔍 Part 2: Understanding Key Words

Q3. Read the sentence and answer the question: What does “All” refer to?

"All are important and must be reasonably satisfied if we are to fulfill our biological destiny."
  • A. All human beings
  • B. All five basic human needs mentioned earlier
  • C. All biological processes
  • D. All living creatures
🧑‍🏫 Detailed Explanation:
A ❌：对象错误 | B ✅：回指上文五种需求 | C ❌：范围错误 | D ❌：扩大范围

Q4. What does “reasonably” most nearly mean?

"All are important and must be reasonably satisfied if we are to fulfill our biological destiny."
  • A. Completely
  • B. Excessively
  • C. In a balanced and moderate way
  • D. Quickly
🧑‍🏫 Detailed Explanation:
A ❌：误解为“完全满足” | B ❌：与原意相反 | C ✅：适度、合理 | D ❌：无关

🔬 Part 3: Deep Dive into "Biological Destiny"

Q5. What does “biological” most nearly mean?

"All are important and must be reasonably satisfied if we are to fulfill our biological destiny."
  • A. related to society and culture
  • B. related to the body and natural life processes
  • C. related to success
  • D. related to education
🧑‍🏫 Explanation: A ❌：这是 social | B ✅：与生命本能及身体自然过程相关 | C, D ❌：完全无关

Q6. What does “destiny” most nearly mean?

"All are important and must be reasonably satisfied if we are to fulfill our biological destiny."
  • A. something we choose freely
  • B. something unimportant
  • C. a natural or expected purpose
  • D. a short-term plan
🧑‍🏫 Explanation: A ❌：destiny 不等于自由选择 | C ✅：指自然赋予或预期的终极目标 | D ❌：非短期计划

Q7. Which of the following BEST explains the combination “biological destiny”?

"All are important and must be reasonably satisfied if we are to fulfill our biological destiny."
  • A. A person’s career goal
  • B. A natural purpose related to human life
  • C. A social responsibility
  • D. A personal dream
🧑‍🏫 Explanation: A, D ❌：过于具体或主观 | B ✅：完美诠释了作为生命体所对应繁衍与生存的自然目标 | C ❌：属于社会学而非生物学

Q8. Which sentence best expresses the full meaning of “fulfill our biological destiny”?

"All are important and must be reasonably satisfied if we are to fulfill our biological destiny."
  • A. To become successful
  • B. To achieve our natural role as human beings
  • C. To control everything
  • D. To avoid problems
🧑‍🏫 Explanation: B ✅：不仅活下去，还要完整实现作为人类应当扮演的自然角色。A, C, D 均属于偏离原意。

🔄 Part 4: Sentence Structure

Q9. What is the logical structure of this sentence?

"All are important and must be reasonably satisfied if we are to fulfill our biological destiny."
  • A. Cause → Effect
  • B. Condition → Result
  • C. Comparison
  • D. Contrast
🧑‍🏫 Explanation: B ✅：由 "if" 引导的是必要条件，主句展现的是在此条件下产生的结果。

Q10. Choose the correct grammatical division (sense group) of the sentence:

"All are important and must be reasonably satisfied if we are to fulfill our biological destiny."
  • A. All are important / and must be reasonably satisfied if we are
  • B. All are important and must be reasonably satisfied / if we are to fulfill our biological destiny
  • C. All are important and must be / reasonably satisfied if we are to fulfill our biological destiny
  • D. All are important and must be reasonably satisfied if we are / to fulfill our biological destiny
🧑‍🏫 Explanation: B ✅：前半段为完整的主句意群，后半段为独立的 if 条件状语从句，在此处断句最符合语法和逻辑。

✍️ Part 5: Vocabulary Substitution

Q11. Which words can replace “important” in an academic context? (Choose TWO options)

"All are important and must be reasonably satisfied if we are to fulfill our biological destiny."
  • A. essential
  • B. unnecessary
  • C. significant
  • D. optional
🧑‍🏫 Explanation: A, C ✅：均为 important 的优秀替换词。B ❌反义，D ❌减弱了原句的必要性程度。

Q12. Which phrase can best replace “must be reasonably satisfied”?

"All are important and must be reasonably satisfied if we are to fulfill our biological destiny."
  • A. must be completely fulfilled
  • B. must be ignored
  • C. must be met in a balanced way
  • D. must be quickly finished
🧑‍🏫 Explanation: C ✅：最精准。A ❌错在 completely（过满），B, D 均不符合原意。

Q13. In terms of contextual meaning, which phrase is closest to “biological destiny”?

"All are important and must be reasonably satisfied if we are to fulfill our biological destiny."
  • A. live a normal human life
  • B. earn money
  • C. gain power
  • D. avoid duties
🧑‍🏫 Explanation: A ✅：符合人类作为高级生物应当享有的完整生命状态。其他选项均偏离或只是其中一个子集。

🧩 Part 6: Formality and Register

Q14. Which expressions can replace “fulfill our biological destiny”? (Choose THREE options)

Q15. Which of the following is the most INFORMAL (conversational) expression?

  • A. fulfill our biological destiny
  • B. achieve our natural purpose
  • C. live a normal human life
  • D. complete our biological mission
🧑‍🏫 Explanation: C ✅：用词直白（live, normal），最适合口语交流，属于常见通俗语体。

Q16. Which of the following is the most FORMAL / Academic expression?

"All are important and must be reasonably satisfied if we are to fulfill our biological destiny."
  • A. live a normal human life
  • B. follow instincts
  • C. fulfill our biological destiny
  • D. get what we want
🧑‍🏫 Explanation: C ✅：原句用词极其高级（fulfill, biological destiny），学术色彩和书面文学色彩最浓。

Q17. Match the expressions with their correct register: (1) live a normal human life [A. informal] | (2) achieve our natural purpose [B. neutral] | (3) fulfill our biological destiny [C. formal]. Choose the correct match option:

🧠 Part 7: Guided Paraphrasing Practice

Q18. Cloze Test: Choose the option that correctly completes this paraphrase framework:
"All these needs are ________ and should be ________ satisfied if we want to ________."

"All are important and must be reasonably satisfied if we are to fulfill our biological destiny."
  • A. important / reasonably / live a normal human life
  • B. optional / fully / become powerful
  • C. unnecessary / quickly / survive
  • D. different / completely / be successful
🧑‍🏫 Explanation: A ✅：符合句意底层逻辑。B、C、D 填入后均大范围扭曲或窄化了原句的意思。

Q19. Sentence Transformation: Which sentence successfully rewrites the original structure using "in order to" without grammar mistakes?

"All are important and must be reasonably satisfied if we are to fulfill our biological destiny."
  • A. All needs are important in order to they are satisfied.
  • B. All needs must be reasonably satisfied in order to fulfill our biological destiny.
  • C. In order to all needs are important, we must satisfy them.
  • D. All needs must be satisfied in order to they are important.
🧑‍🏫 Explanation: B ✅：结构完美，"in order to" 后面顺利接上动词原形短语充当目的状语。A, C, D 均存在严重的 "in order to" 后错接从句的语法错误。

✨ Part 8: Capstone Competence Evaluation

Q20. Which of the following represents the BEST and most accurate paraphrase of the original sentence?

"All are important and must be reasonably satisfied if we are to fulfill our biological destiny."
  • A. All needs must be fully satisfied.
  • B. Every need is important and should be met in a balanced way for humans to live properly.
  • C. Humans only need some needs.
  • D. Needs are not important.
🧑‍🏫 Explanation: B ✅：核心词汇全部完美替换，句式重组恰当，语义完全等值。A, C, D 均信息残缺或错误。

Q21. Error Analysis: Which of the following paraphrases is NOT correct (distorts the original meaning)?

"All are important and must be reasonably satisfied if we are to fulfill our biological destiny."
  • A. All needs should be satisfied in a reasonable way.
  • B. All needs should be met excessively.
  • C. Needs must be balanced.
  • D. Every need matters.
🧑‍🏫 Explanation: B ❌：题目要求选出错误改写。excessively（过度地）直接违背了原句的 reasonably（合理适度），因此是绝对错误的改写。
